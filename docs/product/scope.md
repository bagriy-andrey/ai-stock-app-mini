# Scope

## In Scope

- Real portfolio tracking
- Watchlist management
- Market data ingestion
- Long-term investment analysis
- Tactical/speculative analysis
- Opportunity discovery
- Multi-agent AI analysis
- Structured forecasts
- Prediction storage
- Outcome evaluation
- Reports and alerts
- Telegram notifications
- AI Paper Trader with virtual money
- Agent and model performance tracking
- OpenRouter-based model routing
- AI usage and cost tracking

## Out of Scope for Early Phases

- Real brokerage execution
- High-frequency trading
- Reinforcement learning
- Model fine-tuning
- Highly complex ML pipelines
- Microservice-heavy architecture
- Many notification channels
- Hundreds of data providers
- Minute-by-minute LLM calls for every asset

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

- The real portfolio is advisory-only.
- Paper Trader is isolated from the real portfolio.
- Forecasts and evaluations must preserve point-in-time correctness.
- Important outputs should be structured and persisted.
- MVP phases should stay simple and measurable.
